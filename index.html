Migration: The Threads That Connect Us

Migration is moving to live in a new area or country, often for work, safety, or better living conditions.


Immigration the act of moving from one home country to live and settle in a new foreign country. Short visits like a vacation or a business trip do not count as immigration.


Common push factors include poverty, high unemployment, war and armed conflict, political oppression or lack of freedom, religious persecution, and natural disasters or climate change.


Many global and local organizations help migrants and refugees by providing legal aid, food, housing, and medical care. Key international groups include the International Organization for Migration (IOM) and the UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR).
